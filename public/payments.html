Publisher Payments

Understand how your application earns money from eligible advertising activity, how daily request limits work, and how to monitor your earnings.

1. How publisher payments work

When your application displays advertisements through ZambianAdvertisements, eligible user activity can generate earnings for your registered application.

Your application earns from verified advertising clicks and views. These earnings are calculated in Zambian Kwacha (ZMW) and credited to your publisher account through the settlement system.

1
Render advertisements Integrate the SDK and display ads in your application.
2
User activity Users interact with advertisements through eligible views and clicks.
3
Calculate earnings Verified activity is considered under the applicable payment rates and daily limits.
4
Settlement Eligible earnings are transferred to your publisher account balance.

2. Current advertising payment rates

The settlement rates currently configured for publisher earnings are shown below.

Eligible advertising click
K15.00
Credited for each eligible, verified click.
Eligible advertising view
K0.03
Credited for each eligible, verified view.
Example: If one payment entry contains both an eligible click and an eligible view, the current settlement calculation adds K15.00 and K0.03, giving K15.03.

These are the rates configured in the current settlement procedure. They are not a guarantee that every browser request will generate earnings.

3. Daily advertising request limits

Each publisher application is subject to a daily eligible advertising request limit. This limit is determined using the proven active users of the publisher's application.

The limit is intended to reflect the actual active audience of your application. It is not automatically the same for every publisher.

How the daily limit is determined

  • The network assesses the proven active users of your application.
  • Your application's daily eligible request allowance is based on that active-user information.
  • Applications with different proven active user numbers may receive different daily limits.
  • The limit applies to eligible requests within the applicable day.
Important: Requests exceeding your assigned daily limit will not qualify for additional publisher earnings. Reaching the limit means that further requests for that day are not payable.

Example of a daily limit

Suppose your application is assigned a limit of 500 eligible advertising requests per day.

Request range Payment eligibility
Requests 1–500 Eligible for payment, subject to verification and other payment conditions.
Request 501 onwards Not eligible for additional publisher earnings for that day.
The 500 requests shown above are an example, not a fixed allowance for every application. Your actual daily limit depends on your application's proven active users and the allowance assigned to it.

4. Why daily limits matter

Daily limits help align payable advertising activity with the actual audience of each publisher application.

Your daily allowance should therefore be understood before deploying the advertising integration across your application.

  • Do not assume that every request made by your application will qualify for payment.
  • Do not assume that another publisher's daily limit will apply to your application.
  • Your allowance is based on proven active users, rather than simply the number of registered accounts.
  • Advertising activity beyond the assigned daily allowance will not generate additional payable earnings.

5. Your publisher earnings dashboard

Publishers will be provided with a dashboard for monitoring advertising activity and earnings.

The dashboard will help your technical and management teams understand how the application is performing and how much it has earned.

Information available on your dashboard

Dashboard information What it shows
Daily activity Advertising activity recorded for each day.
Eligible requests Requests counted within your assigned daily allowance.
Clicks and views Eligible clicks and views recorded by the system.
Daily earnings The amount earned from eligible user activity each day.
Account balance Your publisher earnings credited to your account.
Settlement records Payments that have been processed and credited.
Why use the dashboard? You can review the days your application generated advertising activity, compare daily earnings, and monitor the amount credited to your publisher account.

6. When are earnings credited?

Advertising earnings are processed through the network's settlement system. Eligible payment entries are assessed before the corresponding amount is deducted from the advertising company's available balance and added to your publisher account balance.

Settlement is subject to the following conditions:

  • The publisher must have a registered account balance.
  • The advertising activity must have an eligible matching advertisement and company account.
  • The advertising company must not be blocked.
  • The advertising company must have sufficient available funds for the payment.
  • The payment entry must qualify under the application's eligibility and daily limit rules.
An activity record does not automatically mean that its earnings have already been credited. The settlement system must successfully process the payment.

7. Important information for integration teams

  • Use the official ZambianAdvertisements SDK when displaying advertisements.
  • Request a valid authorization token through your backend before initializing the SDK.
  • Do not attempt to create artificial advertising activity or manipulate request counts.
  • Build your integration so that advertising failures do not interrupt the normal operation of your application.
  • Review the assigned daily limit before estimating the potential advertising earnings of your application.
  • Use your publisher dashboard to monitor activity and credited earnings.
Payment eligibility: Only activity accepted by the network's verification, eligibility and settlement processes should be considered payable. Requests above the assigned daily limit are not payable.
