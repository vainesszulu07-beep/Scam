How Payments Work

Learn how your application earns from advertisements, how daily request limits work, and how to monitor the funds generated by user activity.

1. How your application earns

When your application displays advertisements through ZambianAdvertisements, eligible user activity can generate earnings for your registered application.

Earnings are calculated from recorded and eligible advertisement clicks and views.

Eligible advertisement click
15.00
Credited for each eligible recorded click.
Eligible advertisement view
0.03
Credited for each eligible recorded view.
Important: These are the rates currently represented in the settlement calculation. The values are shown without a currency symbol; the applicable account currency should be confirmed with the advertising network.

2. Example of how earnings are calculated

The system calculates earnings using the eligible click and view activity recorded for your application.

Eligible clicks 2 × 15.00 = 30.00
Eligible views 100 × 0.03 = 3.00
Total earnings 33.00

This is an illustration, not a guaranteed amount. Actual earnings depend on the activity that is recorded, verified and accepted for payment.

3. Daily advertisement request limits

Each registered application is subject to a daily advertisement request limit. The limit determines how many qualifying advertisement requests can generate payable activity within a daily reporting period.

Example daily limit
500 requests
Example only — not a guaranteed limit for every app.

Suppose your application has a daily limit of 500 qualifying advertisement requests.

Requests within that limit may qualify for payment, provided the activity also passes the network's eligibility checks.

Once the limit has been exceeded, additional requests during that daily period will not generate payment.

Example

Daily request limit 500
Requests received 650
Requests beyond the limit 150
Additional requests Not payable
Please take note: The daily limit applies to qualifying advertisement requests, not a guarantee that every request will earn money. Click and view activity must still be eligible and successfully recorded.

Your actual daily limit will depend on the limit assigned to your application.

4. When are earnings credited?

Recorded activity is processed by the payment settlement system. The system checks the application and the advertiser's account before transferring earnings to your publisher account balance.

An activity record may be skipped when the payment conditions are not satisfied. Examples include:

  • Your publisher account does not have a valid registered balance account.
  • Your application is not eligible for settlement.
  • The advertisement cannot be matched to exactly one registered advertiser.
  • The advertiser's account is blocked.
  • The advertiser does not have sufficient funds to cover the payment.
How the balance works: When a payment is successfully settled, the advertiser's account balance is deducted and the corresponding amount is added to your publisher account balance.

Crediting your publisher account balance is not the same as an automatic mobile-money or bank transfer. Any withdrawal or payout process is handled separately according to the network's payment arrangements.

5. Your publisher dashboard

Registered applications will be provided with a dashboard where publishers can monitor the activity and funds generated by their advertisements.

The dashboard is intended to help you understand how your application is performing over different days.

Daily activity View advertisement activity recorded on individual days.
Daily earnings See the funds generated by eligible user activity each day.
Clicks and views Monitor eligible clicks and views recorded for your app.
Account balance Monitor earnings credited to your publisher account.
Daily request usage Monitor how much of your assigned daily request limit has been used.
Payment records Review the payment activity processed for your application.
Your dashboard will help you follow your application's performance and understand how much its user activity generates over time.

6. What happens after you register?

  1. Submit your application request Provide the required details about the application you want to connect to the advertising network.
  2. Application review Your submitted application details will be reviewed as part of the registration process.
  3. Receive your integration credentials Once approved, you can obtain the credentials required to connect your application.
  4. Integrate the advertising SDK Follow the frontend integration guide to display advertisements in your application.
  5. Monitor your earnings Use your publisher dashboard to monitor eligible activity and funds generated.
