ADVERTISER INFORMATION

1. What is your role as an advertiser?

As an advertiser, your role is to provide the advertising campaigns you want displayed to users through applications participating in the ZambianAdvertisements network.

Your advertising campaigns are made available to participating publishers. When eligible advertising activity takes place, the corresponding amount is deducted from your advertising account balance through the network's payment settlement process.

This allows your business or organisation to advertise through digital applications while publishers earn from eligible activity generated through their platforms.

Your role: Fund your advertising account, provide your advertising materials and monitor your advertising expenditure through your advertiser dashboard.

2. How your advertising money is deducted

Advertising deductions are calculated using eligible, verified user activity. The current payment rates configured in the network's settlement system are:

Eligible advertising click
K15.00
Deducted for an eligible, verified click.
Eligible advertising view
K0.03
Deducted for an eligible, verified view.
Example: If one payment entry contains both an eligible click and an eligible view, the current settlement calculation deducts K15.00 + K0.03 = K15.03.

These rates reflect the current publisher settlement configuration. The applicable advertising charges and campaign terms should be confirmed before a campaign is activated.

3. How the deduction process works

Your account is connected to the advertising activity generated through participating applications. Eligible payment entries are processed by the settlement system.

1
Advertising account Your available advertising funds are recorded in your company account.
2
User activity Users generate eligible advertising views and clicks through participating applications.
3
Verification Eligible payment records are checked before settlement.
4
Balance deduction The corresponding amount is deducted and credited to the publisher.
Not every raw request automatically creates a charge. The payment must qualify under the network's verification, eligibility and settlement requirements before it is processed.

6. Protection against excessive deductions

We understand that advertisers need confidence that their available funds are protected.

The settlement process is designed with account-balance checks before eligible publisher payments are deducted. The intention is to prevent deductions from exceeding the available advertising balance through this process.

How the balance protection works

  • The system checks the company's available balance before processing a publisher payment.
  • A payment is not processed when the available balance is insufficient to cover the amount.
  • The settlement process does not deliberately allow an eligible payment deduction to push the account balance below zero.
  • When the company balance reaches zero, the system can mark the company account as blocked.
  • Blocked company accounts are not used to process further publisher payments.
Your funds matter. Our settlement logic includes balance checks and account-blocking controls designed to prevent uncontrolled deductions through the publisher payment process.
These protections apply to the described settlement process. They should not be interpreted as a guarantee against every possible loss, system error, unauthorised transaction or other financial risk.

7. Advertising activity and eligibility

Charges are based on eligible activity accepted by the network. The number of raw requests alone does not determine the amount deducted from your account.

  • Advertising activity must pass the applicable verification and eligibility checks.
  • Publisher payment records must be associated with a valid advertising company and advertisement.
  • The company must have sufficient available funds for the payment being processed.
  • Payments associated with blocked companies are not processed.
  • The dashboard is intended to help you monitor the resulting account activity.
Only verified, eligible and successfully settled advertising activity should be treated as a completed charge.
