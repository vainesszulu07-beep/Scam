Welcome to ZambianAdvertisements

A developing advertising platform with a focus on connecting Zambian publishers, application developers and advertisers through digital advertising.

BUILT WITH ZAMBIA IN MIND

1. What is ZambianAdvertisements?

ZambianAdvertisements is a developing digital advertising platform designed specifically for the Zambian market. Our aim is to create an opportunity for local application developers and publishers to earn from advertising while helping businesses reach audiences through digital platforms.

We want to make digital advertising more accessible to Zambian publishers and advertisers by providing a platform where both sides can connect and participate in the advertising ecosystem.

Our focus is on building a system that considers the needs of Zambian businesses, developers, publishers and digital audiences.

Our focus: Creating more opportunities for digital advertising within Zambia and supporting the growth of the local digital technology ecosystem.

2. Designed for Zambia

ZambianAdvertisements is specifically focused on serving the Zambian market. Our platform is being developed with local businesses, local publishers and Zambian audiences in mind.

Supporting the Zambian digital community

  • Helping Zambian application developers and publishers access advertising opportunities.
  • Giving Zambian businesses another way to promote their products and services digitally.
  • Encouraging the development of digital platforms and local technology projects.
  • Building an advertising network that understands the needs of the Zambian market.

Our long-term aim is to contribute to a stronger local digital advertising environment where Zambian businesses and developers can participate.

3. What does the platform offer?

ZambianAdvertisements is being developed around two main groups: publishers and advertisers.

▣

Publisher opportunities

An opportunity for application developers and publishers to display advertisements and earn from eligible, verified advertising activity.

◈

Business advertising

A platform through which businesses can seek digital advertising opportunities and reach users through participating applications.

▤

Activity and payments

Publisher payment tracking and advertising activity information are part of the platform's developing ecosystem.

5. Our direction and long-term vision

Our vision is to develop a digital advertising network that creates meaningful opportunities for Zambian publishers and businesses.

We want to make it easier for local publishers to participate in advertising while creating another digital marketing option for businesses looking to reach people in Zambia.

As development progresses, our aim is to improve the platform, expand participation and build relationships with individuals and organisations that support technology and digital innovation in Zambia.

6. Current organisational status

We believe in being open about the current stage of ZambianAdvertisements.

Registration disclosure:

ZambianAdvertisements is currently a developing advertising platform and is not yet registered as a company in Zambia.

We do not claim to be a registered Zambian company. Our organisational and registration arrangements are still part of our development journey.

We are sharing this information so that publishers, advertisers, potential supporters and other interested individuals understand the platform's current status before choosing to participate.
